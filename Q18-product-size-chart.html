How to Choose Your Size

  1. Measure your chest around the fullest part.
  2. Measure shoulder width from one shoulder edge to the other.
  3. Measure the garment length from the highest shoulder point.
  4. Compare your measurements with the size chart.
  5. Choose the fit you prefer, allowing for the manufacturer's sizing differences.

Product Care Instructions

  • Read the garment care label before washing.
  • Wash with similar colours.
  • Use a suitable detergent.
  • Avoid excessive heat when drying.
  • Follow the manufacturer's ironing instructions.
